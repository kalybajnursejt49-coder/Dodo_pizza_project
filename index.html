About the Location

This is an educational website created for the Web Technology course by a team of three students. The website is dedicated to the Dodo Pizza location at 47 Mangilik El Avenue. We chose this location because it is close to us, allowing us to visit it and take photos for the website. The website provides information about the menu, prices, address, opening hours, project team, and development process. The advantages of this location are its convenient location, pleasant interior, and friendly staff. Some disadvantages are that the pizza can sometimes be greasy and order mistakes may occur. Overall, it is a good location with tasty pizza and good service. All information was obtained from the official Dodo Pizza website and from our personal visit.

Why We Chose Dodo Pizza

We chose this location because it offers AITU students a 15% discount.

Fact: The name comes from the extinct Mauritian dodo, reflecting the founder's idea that the business should be completely open and honest.
