Interesting Facts

Dodo Pizza was founded by Fyodor Ovchinnikov in 2011 in Syktyvkar. Today, the chain has locations in dozens of countries, including Kazakhstan. Several Dodo Pizza locations operate in Astana, each serving its own delivery area.

The company uses its own technology platform to track orders from the oven to the courier1.

1 According to the company's official website.
